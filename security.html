Protect Your Information

Everything you need to know to use Hello Premium safely.

🛡️

The one rule that matters most

We will never ask you to provide your banking password, ATM PIN, OTP, CVV or full card number — on this platform, by phone, or on WhatsApp. No legitimate bank or support service will ever need these from you either.

01

Never share your OTP

One-time passcodes are for you alone, even if someone claims to be your bank.

02

Never share your ATM PIN

No support agent, including ours, ever needs this number.

03

Never share your password

Use official bank or platform apps to log in — always.

04

Never share your CVV

The 3-digit code on your card should stay with you only.

05

Never share a full card number

Legitimate transactions rarely require reading out your full card number to a third party.

06

Use official channels

Always complete authentication and payments on your bank or platform's own website/app.
